A fun afternoon project to see how a simple language model would handle a tarot reading. Draw a past, present, and future spread from a full Rider-Waite deck, flip the cards, and an OpenAI model writes an interpretation of the three together.

I had plans to make it more elaborate, but it ended up just being an enjoyable afternoon and a quick look at how well a small model can riff on a structured prompt.
