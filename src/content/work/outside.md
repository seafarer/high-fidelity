**At a glance**
- Role: Internal engineering lead and product owner, publishing platform
- Timeline: 2019–2022
- Scale: 30 site migrations from three acquisitions, 36 properties, ~5M monthly visitors
- Stack: WordPress multisite, Twig, CircleCI, WordPress VIP, Pantheon, Python import tooling

**The situation**
Outside was growing fast through acquisitions, bringing in outdoor and adventure publications that each ran on their own stack with their own editorial habits. When I joined, the company had around 45 people and its "core four" websites were in various states of quality. Within a couple of years that headcount had grown dramatically, and the network needed a platform that could absorb new properties quickly without multiplying engineering and hosting costs.

**What I did**
I pushed for a WordPress multisite architecture early and served as the in-house engineering owner as it came together, working alongside an external design systems team that built out the shared theme layer.

- **Migration tooling.** Built the content import mechanics with an internal Python developer, which carried 30 sites from three acquisitions onto the platform in under 18 months.
- **Developer experience.** Set up the local development environment and Bash tooling the team used day to day.
- **DevOps and hosting.** Owned CircleCI pipelines, deployment, and the WordPress VIP relationship, then led the platform's move to Pantheon when VIP stopped being the right fit.
- **Platform ownership.** Acted as day-to-day product owner: running triage across outages, PHP and WordPress issues, and the internal ticket queue, and coordinating with the teams responsible for the membership paywall, video, analytics, and consent tooling.

**Outside Watch**
Alongside the platform work, I led a cross-functional team of engineers and designers to build and launch watch.outsideonline.com, a greenfield React SPA that drove new subscriptions. I owned requirements, vision, and roadmap with stakeholders and ran the team's Agile process.

**The result**
A single publishing platform supporting 36 properties and roughly 5 million monthly visitors, with shared infrastructure and a repeatable path for bringing new acquisitions online.
