A UI for quickly building video prompts. Rather than writing one long paragraph, you fill in the parts video models respond to: scene and characters, camera and lens, lighting and mood, a list of actions, and background audio and dialogue. The formatted prompt updates live, copies with one click, and can be saved in the browser to reuse later.

I took the site down when Sora shut down. The structure isn't really specific to Sora, though, and I may rebuild it as a general video-prompt tool.
