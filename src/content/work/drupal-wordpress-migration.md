A publisher's Drupal platform had reached end-of-life: no security updates, a shrinking pool of Drupal talent, and content locked behind a content model no one still fully understood. The task was to move roughly 25,000 posts, spanning years of inconsistent field usage and ad hoc content types, onto WordPress without losing structure, media, or URLs.

Rather than a manual field-mapping slog, I built AI-assisted migration tooling to classify content types, reconcile inconsistent taxonomies, and flag edge cases for human review, paired with automated validation that compared source and destination content before and after each migration batch. That combination caught problems early instead of after launch, and let the project land cleanly and roughly 30% under the original budget.
