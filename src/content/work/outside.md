Outside runs a network of endemic outdoor and adventure publications, properties that started as separate, individually-run WordPress installs with no shared infrastructure, no consistent editorial tooling, and no path to scale.

Over three years I led the engineering and product work that consolidated the network onto a single WordPress multisite architecture: shared themes and plugins, a common editorial workflow, centralized DevOps and deploy pipelines, and a migration path that brought each property onto the platform without disrupting publishing. The network grew from 4 properties to 36, serving roughly 5 million monthly visitors, while the per-site engineering and hosting overhead dropped sharply.

This was as much product ownership as engineering: prioritizing which properties migrated when, working with editorial teams on what the new platform needed to support, and making the platform's constraints legible to non-technical stakeholders.
